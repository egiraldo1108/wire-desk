Keep it playing while you read

Tap the video, then the picture-in-picture button in the player controls. The stream pops out and floats over everything, so it keeps running while you open articles. A page can't start that itself — iOS only allows it from your tap — but once it's floating it survives you leaving the page entirely.

Getting a link for any channel

Open the channel on YouTube, click its live stream, copy the address bar. Works with watch?v=…, youtu.be/…, /live/…, or a /channel/UC… URL — a channel link auto-follows whatever they're streaming right now, so it never goes stale. Free 24/7 news channels: Sky News, DW News, France 24 English, ABC News Live, CBS News, NBC News NOW, LiveNOW from FOX, Bloomberg.

Where to find feed links

Try thesite.com/feed or /rss. Any subreddit: add .rss to its URL. YouTube: youtube.com/feeds/videos.xml?channel_id=…. Any topic at all: news.google.com/rss/search?q=your+terms.

Feed health

Free proxies throttle hard. A Cloudflare Worker of your own takes ten minutes and ends this permanently — the README has the ten lines of code.

Why does one say "fail"?

Your browser only reads another site's data if that site says it's OK, and most news sites never turned that on. So the page asks a free middleman — a relay — to fetch it instead. A fail is almost always the relay being busy, not the news site. Refresh usually fixes it. The README has a 10-minute setup for your own relay.
